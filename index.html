Creative disciplines

Six disciplines. One collective.

All six are welcome. Early programming will focus on literary, visual, and digital arts. Members can choose more than one discipline, so interdisciplinary creators are never forced into a single box.

  • Literary & Publishing Arts

    Authors, poets, playwrights, editors, and independent publishers.

  • Photography & Visual Arts

  • Design & Digital Media

    Graphic designers, digital artists, motion designers, and web creatives.

  • Film & Media Production

  • Music & Performing Arts

    Musicians, composers, actors, and dancers.

  • Fashion & Creative Craft

    Fashion designers, textile artists, makers, and stylists.

Membership

Founding Artist Member.

During the pilot there is one membership category, and it is free. It is open to LGBTQIA+ artists and to allies who support the mission and community standards. Emerging, independent, and established creators are all welcome.

There are no dues, no required donations, no participation quotas, and no portfolio or resume requirement. You do not need to disclose your sexual orientation or gender identity, or prove it.

Where membership starts

  • Artist directoryAn optional public profile with your artist name, discipline, biography, work, and approved links.
  • Creative connectionsFind other members and make mutually agreed introductions or direct contact.
  • Editorial considerationSubmit work or stories for possible spotlights. Features are not guaranteed.

We are also exploring an opportunities board, a digital arts journal, anthologies, exhibitions, workshops, mentorship, partnerships, and collaborative projects. None of these are promised yet.

Editorial priority

Vibe & Vision Collective centers LGBTQIA+ creative voices. While artists and allies are welcome to participate, our editorial programming prioritizes LGBTQIA+ artists and stories when opportunities or available space are limited. Selection also considers artistic relevance, project fit, consent, and available resources.

Joining during the founding period earns the Founding Artist title. It does not mean ownership, a role in governance, guaranteed work, or permanent special privileges. Membership does not guarantee payment, publicity, or professional opportunities.

Founding artists

Your art has a voice. Find your people.

We are beginning with a small group of founding artists who will help shape the culture and direction of the collective. Invitations come first. Public applications open after the pilot, once our systems are ready.

Applications are closed Joining is by invitation for now. If you have received a personal invitation, use the unique link in your email. Public applications will be announced here.

How joining works

  1. Invitation

    Founding artists are invited privately, starting with creative contacts in Las Vegas and artists nationwide. An invitation is not automatic acceptance.

  2. Short application

    Every invitee completes the same application. It is followed by a simple alignment and safety review.

  3. Welcome

    Approved members receive a welcome guide and an invitation to a virtual founding orientation. Orientation is encouraged and never required.

  4. Pilot and feedback

    Founding members help test the directory and onboarding. Public applications follow when the essentials work well.

What the application asks

  • About youPreferred or artist name, email, city and state, and an optional website or social link.
  • Your creative practiceOne or more disciplines and a short description of your work.
  • ConnectionWhat interests you about joining, plus optional interests such as finding collaborators.
  • Member directoryYes, maybe later, or no. Nothing is published without your separate approval.
  • Community agreementRespect for LGBTQIA+ members, creative ownership, and others' boundaries.

Applicants must be 18 or older. A separate, supervised pathway for younger creatives is being researched and is not open.
