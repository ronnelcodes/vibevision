The collective

Made by artists, for artists.

Vibe & Vision Collective brings together artists who are part of the LGBTQIA+ community. Allies are welcome too.

The collective is new. Its first members will help shape what it becomes, so the people who join now matter more than any plan we could write ahead of them.
